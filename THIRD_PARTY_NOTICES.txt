regexdata.json (the Regex page's tablet, waystone and relic mods, built by tools/build_regexdata.py) and searchdata.json
(the Game search window, tools/build_searchdata.py) come from RePoE's
Path of Exile 2 export (https://github.com/repoe-fork/poe2, from https://github.com/repoe-fork/repoe-fork). RePoE's code
is MIT licensed (Copyright (c) 2016 brather1ng). In its own words: "Contents of generated files (all files in the
`data` directory) are owned by Grinding Gear Games and shall not be used or published without being in accordance with
their terms of use." The search strings themselves are generated by StashOfExile2's own code (soe2/regex.py).

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
cxnames.json (Exchange Helper's flip check, 2.26.0, tools/build_cxnames.py): the names of the items traded on the Currency
Exchange, by their game id, from RePoE's export (above).

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
tierdata.json (Desecration Helper, 2.81.0, tools/build_tierdata.py): every mod's tiers, value ranges and spawn tags,
the equipment bases and small fingerprints of their pictures, from RePoE's export (above). The game data belongs to
Grinding Gear Games.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
soe2/class_icons.py (item pictures, 3.3.0, tools/build_class_icons.py): links only. Each equipment base's picture is
downloaded when shown, from RePoE's export (above) on GitHub at one fixed commit. The app ships no pictures. The art
belongs to Grinding Gear Games.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
anointdata.json (Game search > Anoints, 2.17.0, tools/build_anointdata.py): the Liquid Emotion names come from RePoE's
export (above); which emotions anoint each notable comes from the passive tree data in Path of Building Community for
Path of Exile 2 (https://github.com/PathOfBuildingCommunity/PathOfBuilding-PoE2, MIT licensed), extracted from the game
files. The game data itself belongs to Grinding Gear Games. Liquid Emotion prices are read live from poe.ninja.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Pictures: the app icon (stashofexile2.ico and the picture inside the app) is an original drawing made for
StashOfExile2. No game art ships with the app (3.1). Game images - item and currency pictures - are loaded from Path
of Exile's own image server (web.poecdn.com) at runtime, through the image links poe.ninja and the trade site send
with their prices, and kept only on your PC (the art folder in your data folder), like a browser's cache. The screen
helpers compare your screen with those pictures, and with pieces of your own screen they learned on your PC (a
window's title, the stack numbers' digits); nothing of it is sent anywhere. Path of Exile 2 is a trademark of Grinding
Gear Games; StashOfExile2 is a fan-made tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Grinding Gear Games.
